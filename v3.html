© 2026 Yenhsing Cheng

With a professional architecture background, my work focuses on exploring how emerging technologies can expand the way architects design and deliver projects.

My experience in architecture, construction, BIM, and project delivery has shaped my interest in developing computational workflows that connect design ideas with real-world building processes. I explore AI-assisted design, generative workflows, and BIM-driven digital twins using tools such as ComfyUI, Claude Code, and other emerging technologies.

Through this approach, I investigate new ways for architects to work with computation, from generating design possibilities to automating complex tasks and improving decision-making throughout the building lifecycle.

My background spans architecture, construction, BIM and project management, from commercial buildings to large-scale transit projects.

Lately I've been building generative design and computational workflows, tools that help designers move faster from ideas to reality. Currently completing an M.S. in Architectural Technologies at SCI-Arc, Los Angeles.
